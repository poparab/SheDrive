What blocks what

One story gates the entire feature. Nothing else — not a screen, not a report, not a settlement — can be finished before the ledger exists.

Every story: scope, origin, status

Origin matters for estimating. A new story is a fresh build. A reopened or rescoped one was closed or removed and has been rewritten — the old estimate, where there was one, no longer describes the work. An extended one keeps its existing points at the product owner's instruction and may need repointing.

Before this can go into a sprint

1. Estimate 37 items. Only #1832 and #1833 carry points, and both were pointed before this work extended them — their scope grew and the numbers did not. Everything else is unestimated. Three of the unestimated items were previously closed or removed (#1764, #1781, #1813) and one was rescoped (#1788): treat all four as new work, not as carry-over.

2. #1645 is Removed but still depended on. #3996 extends the go-online check that story owned, and #3058's completion flow depends on its availability write. No story currently owns that transition. This needs resolving with the API team before #3996 is started, or #3996 has nothing to extend.

3. Two business decisions gate acceptance criteria, not code. Nobody has decided how the cash physically comes back — which of the four settlement channels SheDrive will actually run, or who staffs it — and #1813's criteria cannot be finalised without it. Separately, driver onboarding does not collect a payout destination today, so #4003 adds a field the onboarding flow will also have to ask for.
