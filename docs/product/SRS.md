# SheDrive SRS Working Copy

## Purpose

This file translates the business baseline into structured system requirements for planning, design, and mockup alignment.

## System Surfaces

### Rider App

- authentication and onboarding
- booking
- matching
- active trip
- payment and completion
- rating and history
- safety and SOS

### Driver App

- authentication and onboarding
- online availability
- request handling
- trip execution
- earnings and balances
- safety and SOS

### Admin Portal

- role-based access
- onboarding review
- account management
- live operations
- SOS handling
- pricing and zones
- finance and reconciliation
- reporting and audit

## Requirement Writing Rules

- requirements must be testable
- unknown providers stay marked as assumptions
- permissions must be explicit
- state transitions must be explicit
- user-visible bilingual behavior must be explicit for rider and driver flows

## Current Assumption Areas

- provider integrations are not yet finalized
- exact operating hours are not yet finalized
- launch pricing baseline is not yet finalized
