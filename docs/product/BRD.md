# SheDrive BRD Working Copy

## Purpose

This file is the normalized BRD working copy for ongoing product analysis. It does not replace the original draft files in the repo. It gives the team one stable place to maintain the current business baseline.

## Product Summary

SheDrive is a safety-first ride-hailing service for Cairo and Giza. The service is positioned as premium and trust-led rather than lowest-price. The product promise centers on rider safety, operational visibility, and culturally aligned mobility for women.

## MVP Surfaces

- Rider mobile experience
- Driver mobile experience
- Admin and operations portal

## MVP Business Anchors

- Greater Cairo only
- Daytime-only operation
- Cash and digital payments
- Women-only rider experience
- Bilingual rider and driver UI
- English-only admin portal
