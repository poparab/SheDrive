# Pre-Story Checklist

- Confirm the source backlog item or BRD section.
- Confirm the persona and business value.
- Check `docs/product/open-decisions.md` for unresolved rules.
- Identify adjacent stories and scope boundaries.
- Decide whether the story needs a validation table, grid spec, or UX notes.
- If the destination is Azure DevOps, decide whether description and acceptance criteria need HTML lists or bordered HTML tables.
